# Google Play store listing (English)

## App name (max 30)
Lujain: Learn American English

## Short description (max 80)
American English + U.S. citizenship test prep, with a chat robot and games

## Full description
Lujain helps Arabic speakers learn **real American English**, the way people actually talk at school, at work and on the street. Built for Arab families in the US, kids and adults alike.

🤖 **Chat with Lujain the robot**
Speak or type in real-life situations: introducing yourself, coffee shop, restaurant, airport, shopping, job interview and free talk. Lujain remembers your answers, gently fixes common mistakes, and speaks every reply in an American voice.

🇺🇸 **U.S. citizenship test prep**
• All 128 official civics questions (2025 test) in English and Arabic, with answers and audio
• Practice interview with a robot “USCIS officer” who asks up to 20 questions out loud; 12 correct to pass
• Practice test game, plus the 65/20 questions for older applicants

🎮 **Learning games**
• Q&A: what people ask you at the coffee shop, airport, on the street, at the mall, doctor, bank, school and work
• Matching, sentence builder and word builder
Earn stars ⭐ as you go.

📚 **700+ word dictionary** in 22 categories, including citizenship and government words.

📖 **Phrasebook** for daily life in America, plus Islamic expressions (InshaAllah, Alhamdulillah, Jummah…).

🎤 **Pronunciation practice** with a score.

⭐ **Smart review** of saved words.

🌐 **Translation** Arabic ↔ English and two-person conversation mode (needs internet).

🌍 **Two languages:** use the app in English or Arabic. Americans can use it to learn Levantine Arabic phrases too.

🔒 **Private by design**
No account, no ads, no subscription. Everything runs on your device.

ℹ️ Educational app. Not affiliated with USCIS or the U.S. government. Official source for the civics questions: uscis.gov
